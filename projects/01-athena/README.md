# 01 · Athena

Brand identity for Athena, a makeup brand, with packaging to follow. From designerbriefs Brief 47 (with @designbyhayaa), hashtag **#athenabrief**.

![The brief](brief/designerbriefs-47-athena.png)

## The brief
- **Business:** Athena, a makeup brand
- **Keywords:** beauty, quality, empowerment
- **Deliverables:** brand identity and packaging design
- **Client:** "Athena offers makeup products inspired by ancient beauty secrets, made with quality ingredients for long-lasting results. The brand empowers individuals to express their inner goddess through makeup."

## Direction: marble and gold
Chosen from four directions (the others were an olive-and-owl "modern goddess", a dark bronze "night oracle" and a pearly "ethereal goddess").

**The idea:** Athena as the Parthenon. Marble, gold and perfect proportion. Draft tagline: *Ancient secrets, lasting beauty.*

### Standing out from every other Greek-goddess entry
This is the most common way to read the brief, so two things have to set it apart:

1. **The products are built from temple architecture.** The lipstick is a fluted column with a gold capital and a plinth. The compact is a gilded medallion. Optional: the box lid is cut as a pediment (the triangular gable on a temple front).
2. **Restraint.** Ivory negative space does most of the work. Gold is used only as foil, and sparingly. Marble is a whisper (a few fine veins), never a stock marble photo.

Avoid: statue and bust clip art, Trajan-style "toga party" lettering, and stacking a laurel wreath, a Greek key and a column all on one surface.

## Identity system (working)

### Logo
- **Wordmark:** ATHENA in a Didone (a high-contrast serif, like Didot or Bodoni) in capitals, widely letterspaced. Start at about +250 tracking and adjust by eye.
- **Mark:** a laurel-crowned A monogram: two laurel branches around the A, open at the top.
- **Ideas to explore for the mark:**
  - The A's apex drawn as a pediment
  - The A's crossbar as a laurel twig
  - The A inside a medallion ring (this doubles as the compact lid)
- **Lockups:** stacked (mark over wordmark), horizontal, and the mark alone (compact lids, embossed lipstick bullets, profile picture).

### Palette
| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Parian ivory | #F4EFE6 | Base, packaging body |
| Limestone | #E8E1D6 | Secondary surfaces, backgrounds |
| Marble vein | #CFC7BA | Fine lines, veining |
| Gilt | #C9A45C | Gold foil (logo, borders, caps) |
| Temple ink | #2B2622 | Text |
| Rose madder | #A8474A | Hero product shade, sparing accent |

Gilt is a flat stand-in for foil on screen. For print, it becomes a spot colour (a gold foil block), not a CMYK mix.

### Type
- **Bodoni Moda**: wordmark and headlines
- **Jost**: body text, labels and product names (Marcellus is an alternative for small-caps labels)
- Both are free on Google Fonts under the SIL Open Font License, so they're fine for commercial use.

### Graphic elements
- **Greek-key border:** `identity/greek-key.svg`. One repeat is 11 units wide. Every gap between parallel lines equals 3 stroke widths, so scale it uniformly and never stretch it.
- **Marble veining:** two or three fine lines in Marble vein, drawn by hand with the Pencil tool.
- **Laurel sprig:** a single branch taken from the mark, for small accents.

## Phases
1. **Moodboard** (`reference/`): 10–15 images. Parthenon details and fluted columns, gold leaf, ancient Greek coins and jewellery, luxury Didone brands, and marble-and-gold packaging. Keep only what fits the palette.
2. **Logo exploration:** one Affinity artboard with three mark ideas and three wordmark tests. Pick one.
3. **Refine:** optical letterspacing, check the mark at small sizes (16px on screen and an 8mm emboss), then build the lockups, clear space and minimum size.
4. **System:** palette, type scale, pattern, and the photo and render mood.
5. **Brand board and portfolio slides** (1080 × 1440).
6. **Packaging:** decided later (flat dielines only, or also 3D renders in cel.3d).

## Affinity setup
- Source file: `source/athena-identity.af`. Keep separate artboards for exploration, final logo and brand board.
- Logo artboards: 1000 × 1000px, RGB. Packaging will get its own CMYK document later.
- Export to `exports/`: SVG (preset **SVG (for export)**) for vectors and PNG @2x for previews.
